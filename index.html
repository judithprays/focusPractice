ABOUT

👋 Hey, I'm Judith - I have over 10 years of engineering experience shipping world class projects. And yet...

I was wasting my precious free time in indecision of what to do with it. Should I do that project? Or the other one? Just take a nap?

I read 20+ time management books and did 12 experiments on myself with the goal of increasing satisfaction and productivity with how I spent my time. What I learned really surprised me... and had very little to do with time management 😂

THE PRACTICE

Choose → Focus → Learn → Push Through → Complete → Share → Recover → Choose again

It turns out what I needed to feel more satisfaction and productivity was not more time, or to be better at time, but to be better at choosing a focus.

Focus is a muscle, and this is a practice to improve it.

I'm working on a book that explains the practice, as well as coaching and workshop offerings. Reach out to judithprays@gmail.com for more info!
